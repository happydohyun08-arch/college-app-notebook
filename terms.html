1. The service College App Manager (the 'Service') is a free tool that helps high-school students organize application materials and get essay feedback. It has no payments.

2. Who can use it Ages 14 and up. Users under 14 need a parent's consent and cannot use the Service for now.

3. Your data What you enter is stored only in your browser. Changing devices or clearing browser data can erase it, so keep your own copy with Backup. The operator does not hold your data and cannot restore it.

4. AI features AI feedback and suggestions are sent to Anthropic for processing only when you agree each time. AI results are opinions that can be wrong and do not guarantee or predict admission.

5. Your responsibilities What you submit to colleges must be your own writing. Submitting work by someone else or by AI can break college rules or count as plagiarism. Do not disrupt the Service, for example by hiding instructions to the AI or sending automated bulk requests.

6. Usage limits AI features require a Google sign-in and allow 10 uses per account per day for fair use. Each network and the whole site also have daily limits. Only a hash of your Google account identifier is used to count uses.

7. Counselor sharing (optional) When a student agrees to join a counselor's class, a copy of their school list, checklist, essays and AI scores is saved as a file in the student's own Google Drive and shared read-only with that counselor. The operator's server stores only the class code, the counselor's and student's Google account numbers and emails, and the file id. Students can leave at any time; leaving deletes the file and ends sharing. Counselors must use student data only to advise that student and must not pass it on. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

8. School data School fit and statistics come from public data (IPEDS, Common Data Set) and are only a guide. Check the source and each college's official information.

9. Changes If these terms change, we will ask you to agree again on your next visit.

10. Privacy How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
